Pixel Paint by Numbers

Turn an image into a numbered colour grid you can print and paint.

1. Choose your image

PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF or BMP. Your image stays on your device.

2. Set your grid

Width × height, from 1 to 128 cells. The image is fitted inside the grid without stretching.
Enter 2–256, or use 0 for original colours (no reduction).
The most frequently used colour becomes No. 1. Original keeps every distinct colour in the image.
Changes the on-screen preview only. Downloads keep their full resolution.
Your grid updates automatically as you change these settings.

3. Export your artwork

Choose an image to get started.
